PageProof searching

PageProof runs its searches (Google Lens, Yandex, Bing and owner checks) in the tabs of this window, so your own window stays clear.

Please leave this window minimized. If a search site asks "are you a robot?", PageProof opens it for you. When you are done, minimize it again.

Closed it by mistake? No problem: PageProof opens a new one the next time it searches. You can switch back to pinned tabs in PageProof › Keep searching › Where searches run.
