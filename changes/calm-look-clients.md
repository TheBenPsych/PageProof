title: The calm look, step 12: Clients and photos, with Registrations inside
news: Clients and photos has the new look, and Registrations now lives inside it instead of being its own menu item. The client list has five tiles on top (one opens Registrations, one opens Skipped photos), a tidy command bar, and one calm line per client with new columns for Done by and Registration. Clients that are switched off sit grey at the bottom. Tick clients to get a small floating bar. Press Registrations (it shows how many photos need a number) to open a panel on the right: three tabs, one number box per folder with Save, a find box, a client pick and Copy for the registrar. Registered lately now says where each number was found and when. A client's own page has a back arrow, a Searching switch and a Priority button, four tiles, folders that show which have a VA number, an application or no number, a Registrations card for that client, filters (High priority, Popular, Has copies, Not searched yet, Not searched any more, Doubles), a sort and a picture size. Photos are grouped under their folder. Click a photo to open a new panel with its details, its copies, Search now and Stop searching this photo. Change folder moved into the ⋯ menu.
