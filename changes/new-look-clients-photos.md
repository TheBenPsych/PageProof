title: Clients, photos and site owners in the new look
news: Clients and photos shows a coloured initials badge per client, a plain ★ to make a priority client, and a "Searched once" bar; click anywhere on a row to open that client's photos. The photos page is headed by the client itself (switch client from its name), ⚑ and ★ sit on the corner of each picture, tags are small coloured dots, pictures not loaded yet show a striped tile, and resting the mouse on a photo shows a bigger preview. Site owners get coloured initials, green ticks for why they are one owner, and letter badges on their sites.
