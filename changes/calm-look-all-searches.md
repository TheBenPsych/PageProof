title: The calm look, step 6: All searches
news: All searches has the calm look. A bar at the top has New search, buttons for All, New searches, Today's 50 and Keep searching with a count on each, a tick for only the searches that found copies, and Find a photo or client. The last 7 days show as four tiles and a small chart. A search that is running gets its own card with a progress bar, its photos and Open and Stop. The list has named columns (When, Search, Photos, Sites, Took, Same photo, Maybe, New), a heading for each day with its totals, real thumbnails and plain numbers. Keep searching's one-photo searches open as a grid of photos. Tick the searches you want gone and press one Delete (Undo still brings them back). A search that did not finish says so and has a Search the last N button.
