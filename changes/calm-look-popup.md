title: The calm look, step 11: the pop-up
news: The small window behind the PageProof toolbar button has the new look. Capture this page is now the one blue button and names just the site, with the keyboard shortcut shown on it. The three numbers (New copies, To capture, Searched today) are a tidy list with coloured icons. "Photos on this page" is gone. In its place is a row of five tool buttons: Inspect, Compare, Mark up, Convert and Open file, and they also work on browser pages. Keep searching is one row with its switch on the right and Pause as a small link. Open full PageProof and the light/dark button moved to a bar at the bottom. Warnings are slim bars, and when two show at once they fold to one line each, so the window never grows past what the browser can show (it used to be cut off).
