title: Safety nets: nothing lost, nothing broken in silence
news: PageProof now has room to grow (unlimited browser storage, and a red warning if a save ever fails), backups keep which photos were searched, photos moved or renamed in Google Drive keep their copies and history, a ticked folder that goes missing is flagged on Home, timestamps are retried with two spare services, problems are kept in an error diary for "Save stats for Claude", and every update gets a 1-minute check-up.
timeline: Safety nets: storage, search history in backups, moved photos, timestamp spares, error diary, update check-up
feature 9: **Room to grow.** PageProof asks for unlimited browser storage and keeps its data safe from the browser's clean-up. If a save ever fails, Home and Is everything working say so in red.
feature 9: **Backups keep the search history.** Restoring, or moving to a new computer, carries on where you were instead of searching every photo again.
feature 4: **Moved or renamed photos keep their history.** When a folder is renamed or a shoot is moved in Google Drive, PageProof recognises the photos and keeps their copies, answers, flags and search dates. A ticked folder that can't be found any more gets a red line on Home.
feature 2: **Never short on timestamps.** A timestamp service that doesn't answer is asked again twice, then GlobalSign or Entrust stand in, so every pack gets three trusted timestamps.
feature 9: **Error diary.** Problems PageProof handles quietly are counted by where they happened (with names, links and folders taken out) and listed under What went wrong in Save stats for Claude.
feature 9: **Check-up after every update.** About a minute after a new version opens, PageProof checks the photo checker, opening a Drive photo, saving, the timestamp services and the search sites, and Home says "everything checked" or what broke.
