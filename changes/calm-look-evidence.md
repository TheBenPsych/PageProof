title: The calm look, step 14: All evidence
news: Evidence › All evidence has the new look. Two tabs on top: Captures and Evidence reports. Captures show as bigger picture cards grouped by week and month (or as a list), each with a badge on the picture: green Pack saved, or yellow Pack not saved, No case number or No timestamps. Quick filters next to the find box show only the ones that still need something. Click a capture to open a panel on the right with its case number, who captured it, the pack's fingerprint and trusted time stamps, the Stolen copy it belongs to, and buttons to open the capture, check the pack or save it again. Sort has a new choice, Group: Case number, which puts every capture and report of one case together. Evidence reports show the photo pair and a Match ID you can copy with one click. Needs a capture is no longer its own page, because marking a copy Stolen captures it straight away. Copies that are still waiting show in a bar on top of All evidence, behind the Waiting for a capture filter, on Copies found › Not finished and on Home, each saying how long the oldest has waited. Verify a pack now lives only under Tools, and the Evidence menu item no longer carries a number.
