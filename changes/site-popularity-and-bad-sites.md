title: How popular each site is, and known bad sites
news: Website copies now show how popular their site is (Top 1K, Top 10K, Top 100K, Top 1M), from the free Tranco list of the world's most visited websites. Big sites (top 10K) get a gold tag and a gold edge, and there is a "Big sites" quick filter, a "Site popularity" filter and a "Most popular site" sort. PageProof also checks copies against free lists of known piracy, scam, phishing and malware sites (The Block List Project, URLhaus, OpenPhish): those get a red tag, count as Suspicious, and have their own "Known bad sites" quick filter. The lists download by themselves and are kept on this computer, so no site name is sent anywhere. See them in Settings › Lists.
timeline: Site popularity tags and known bad site lists
feature 5: **How popular the site is.** Website copies get Top 1K / 10K / 100K / 1M tags from the free Tranco list (downloaded weekly, looked up offline). Gold tag and edge for the top 10K, "Big sites" quick filter, Site popularity filter, "Most popular site" sort, World rank in the Download file and a card on the stats page. Social sites, free blog hosts and marketplaces get no rank.
feature 5: **Known bad sites.** Free lists of piracy, scam, phishing and malware sites (The Block List Project, URLhaus, OpenPhish), downloaded by themselves and looked up offline. Red tag, counts as Suspicious, "Known bad sites" quick filter. A top 10K site is only flagged by the piracy list.
