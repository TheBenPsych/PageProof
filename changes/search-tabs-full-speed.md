title: Searches run at full speed in the background, and fewer copies are missed
news: PageProof's search tabs sit in the background, where the browser made every short wait take a full second and never loaded the pictures lower down the results. They now use PageProof's own clock and load every picture, so Google, Yandex and Bing should each answer in about half the time and far fewer links say "No picture". When Google says a page is an exact match but its small preview doesn't line up, PageProof now checks the pictures on that page itself. A post with several pictures (a carousel, an album, TikTok slides) whose first picture is another photo is kept as "Checking the post" for the post check, instead of being thrown out. Plainly different Yandex and Bing links no longer get a slow second look, and a second look reuses the picture already downloaded. The stats file for Claude now has the checker's numbers for every copy you answered (numbers only), so the checker can be tuned on your real answers.
timeline: Search tabs at full speed in the background, every result picture loads, Google exact matches read on their page, multi-picture posts checked, fewer slow second looks
feature 4: **Search tabs at full speed.** The waits inside a background search tab come from PageProof's own clock (a hidden tab's waits are stretched to a second each), and every result picture is told to load straight away, so results lower down the list keep their picture.
feature 4: **Google exact matches read on their page.** When Google lists a page under Exact matches but its small preview doesn't line up, the page's own pictures are checked at full size (up to 10 links a photo).
feature 4: **Posts with several pictures.** A post a search site listed whose cover is another photo is kept as "Checking the post", and the post check looks at every picture in your browser before it stays or goes.
