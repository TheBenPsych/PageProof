title: The calm look, step 15: Evidence › Overview
news: Evidence › Capture a page is now Evidence › Overview. It no longer is a tool, it shows what you have captured: captures this month, evidence packs saved, how many are fully timestamped and how many still wait for a pack, a chart of captures per week for the last 12 weeks, a Needs you list (packs not saved, packs with fewer timestamps, no case number), your newest captures as picture cards, where your captures come from (Stolen copies, the PageProof icon, the keyboard shortcut, right-click), the sites and cases you capture most, how captures went (average time, page length, scroll boxes, pop-ups closed) and how often each trusted time service answered. Capturing itself has not changed: a copy you mark Stolen is still captured by itself, and for any other page you click the PageProof icon › Capture or press the shortcut. The How to capture button on top, and the blue Capture a page button in the top bar, open the three steps and your last tab with Capture this page. From this version on each capture also notes where it was started and how it went, in PageProof's own list only. The capture, the banner and the evidence pack are exactly the same as before.
