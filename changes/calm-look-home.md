title: The calm look, step 10: Home
news: Home has the new look. The four tiles at the top now lead with their number, and two of them say more: how many copies you answered today, and how long the oldest Stolen copy has waited for its capture (red after 3 days). What to do now and Most copied photos sit side by side on one level. Clients and sites are shown as small chips you can click, instead of long sentences. "Since you were last here" is now one folded line, While you were away, with Show and OK. A new Clients board under the jobs shows every client being searched with its new copies and how far its first search is. Click a most copied photo to open a panel on the right with its numbers, its newest copies, a button to see all its copies, and Stop searching this photo. Gone: the Start row of buttons and the "Keep searching is running" line (the tile at the top right already says it).
