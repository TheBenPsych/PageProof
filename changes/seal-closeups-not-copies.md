title: Close-ups of a podium seal no longer count as copies
news: A picture that shows only a seal, crest or emblem from your photo (for example a close-up of the seal on a podium at another event) is no longer called "Maybe the same". It goes to "Thrown out by the checker", where "It is a copy" still brings it back. Real tight crops of your photo still count.
feature 4: **Seal close-ups aren't copies.** A close-up showing only a seal or emblem that also appears in your photo is thrown out.
