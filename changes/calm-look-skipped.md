title: The calm look, step 13: Skipped photos, and TIFF and HEIC photos are searched
news: PageProof now reads TIFF and HEIC photos itself, so they are searched like any other photo. It only reads them: no copy is saved and nothing in Google Drive is changed. Ones skipped before go back to the line by themselves, once. A TIFF or HEIC that is too big or an unusual kind is simply left out. Skipped photos has the new look: four cards on top say why photos were skipped in plain words (TIFF or HEIC, Moved or deleted, Empty file, Won't open) and clicking one filters the list. Under them: a find box, Group, Sort, Download list (a spreadsheet file) and Try all again. Photos are grouped under their client. Tick photos to get a small floating bar. Click a photo for a panel that says what to do, with Try again now, Copy folder path and Take off this list (the file itself is never touched, and Undo puts it back). On Clients and photos, a client with skipped photos shows a small "N skipped" chip that opens this page on that client.
timeline: Calm look step 13: Skipped photos; TIFF and HEIC photos are read and searched (read only)
feature 4: **Calm Skipped photos.** Four reason cards that filter, find box, group by client or flat, sort, Download list (CSV), Try all again, tick boxes with a floating bar, a photo panel (what to do, Copy folder path, Take off this list with Undo) and an "N skipped" chip on each client.
feature 4: **TIFF and HEIC photos are searched.** PageProof reads them in memory with its own readers (read only, nothing is saved or changed in Drive). Files over the size limit are left out.
