title: Everything carries on after an update
news: After an update (Update-PageProof.cmd, then Reload) PageProof now picks up whatever was running. Keep searching, big searches (each from the photo it had reached), Today's 50, a Credit search "Search now" (from the wording it had reached), the weekly folder check (from the client it had reached), sweeps, the watch list and the site lists all carry on by themselves. PageProof pages you had open come back at the same place, so Copies found's post checks, account details and previews carry on too. Anything you paused stays paused. A small "Picked up after the update" note says what carried on.
