title: Check later
news: Not sure about a copy yet? Press Check later (or the C key) in One at a time, the copy details panel, a row's clock button or the ⋯ menu. The copy leaves New and waits in the new Check later tab in Copies found until you answer it. Undo (U) puts it back.

feature 5: **Check later.** A copy you're not sure about goes to its own Check later tab (button or C key), out of the New list, until you answer it. Undo works.
