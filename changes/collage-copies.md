title: Copies inside collages are found
news: A copy that is only part of a bigger picture (your photo side by side with another one, stacked above or below it, in the middle of three panels, or with another photo pasted over one corner) is now called the same photo. Before, the other picture pulled the score down, and a picture-in-picture copy could even be thrown out as "only a seal or emblem matches". PageProof now looks for one straight-edged box where nothing lines up while everything else does, and leaves that box out. Compare shows it as a grey "Added part, left out" box. Seals, crests, press walls and fences are still thrown out. Links you have not answered yet are checked once more.
