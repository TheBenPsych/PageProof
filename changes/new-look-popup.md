title: Pop-up, side panel and capture card in the new look
news: The toolbar pop-up's Capture card now shows the site's letter and the name of the page you're on, with three small tiles under it (new copies, stolen copies to capture, photos searched today) that open those lists. Keep searching is one line with an on/off switch, and Photos on this page shows the first few photos as small pictures (click one to inspect it). The side panel adds a bar for today's searching. While a page is being captured, the card on the website shows four steps and "Screen 6 of 14", has a calmer Stop button and follows your light or dark choice. It is still left out of every screenshot.
