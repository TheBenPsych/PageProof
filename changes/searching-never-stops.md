title: Searching never stops, and new photos go first
news: Keep searching now carries on (at half speed) while Today's 50 or another big search runs, and Yandex and Bing search another photo while Google is still busy. Photos that just arrived in Drive are searched within the hour and again on day 3, 7 and 14. You can set a goal date ("every photo searched once by...") and PageProof works out the pace and says if you're on track. When you mark a copy Stolen, the rest of that shoot is searched next. Near-identical burst frames wait until everything else had its first search. Photos with no year in their folders use the camera's date. Home tells you when new folders (new clients?) appear in Google Drive.
timeline: One search line, per-site lanes, new photo fast lane, goal date, hot shoots, bursts, camera dates, new client folders
feature 4: **One search line.** Big searches no longer stop Keep searching; it carries on at half speed and skips their photos.
feature 4: **Each search site at its own speed.** Google keeps the pace; Yandex and Bing search another photo (a re-search) while Google is busy. Every first search still uses all three.
feature 4: **New photo fast lane.** Photos that arrive in a client PageProof already knows are searched first, then again 3, 7 and 14 days later.
feature 4: **A goal instead of a pace.** Set a date for every photo's first search; PageProof picks the pace (never faster than is safe) and says on track or behind, with the real end date.
feature 4: **Hot shoots.** Marking a copy Stolen sends the other photos from that folder and day to the front of the line (Undo puts them back).
feature 4: **Bursts.** A frame that looks almost the same as one already searched with nothing found waits until every other photo had its first search, and goes at once if its twin finds copies.
feature 4: **Camera dates.** A photo whose folders give no year gets its date from the camera (EXIF).
feature 4: **New client folders.** Once a week PageProof lists the folder names in Google Drive (names only) and Home shows new folders that aren't ticked.
