title: The calm look, step 7: Credit search
news: Credit search is now three short tabs instead of one very long page. Overview shows a search that is running (with a new Stop button), how Google and Bing are doing, four number tiles, the last searches in columns, and a new list of the wordings that find the most copies. Clients lists every client with their wordings as small chips: find a client, show only On, Off or the ones with their own wordings, and click a client to open a panel where you add or remove wordings one at a time. New: Change the usual wordings changes them for every client without their own, in one place. Credit but no photo has its own tab with Open page, Look closer (opens that page's pictures in Inspect a photo) and Take off.
