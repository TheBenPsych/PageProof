title: The calm look, step 1: Windows 11 colours, one font, status at the top right
news: PageProof starts its new calm look. Every page now uses one easy-to-read font (Lexend), the Windows 11 colours in light and a softer grey dark mode, Windows buttons, tick boxes and switches, and a menu with coloured icons and a cogwheel for Settings. Google Drive, Keep searching and Search sites moved from the bottom of the menu to three small tiles at the top right of every page. The pages get their own calmer layouts one by one in the next updates, so for a short while some pages look newer than others.
