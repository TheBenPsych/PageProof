title: Personal accounts hidden, politicians caught more often, link previews are not copies
news: Social media copies from someone's own account (just a person's name, no company, website or contact details: a Facebook profile with friends, a LinkedIn /in/ profile, an X account with only a name) are hidden until you tick "Show personal accounts". Stolen copies always stay, and "Type is wrong?" in About this account sets the type by hand. Politicians are also caught by "serving … District", "(R-Ohio)", committee badges, @Rep… handles and "Rep." in the post title, even before the profile is read. A match that is only the picture in a link preview card (a LinkedIn, X or Facebook post sharing an article) is thrown out during the search, or moved to Removed ("Only in a link preview"; Put back works).
