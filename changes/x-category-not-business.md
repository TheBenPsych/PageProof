title: Personal X accounts are no longer called Business
news: On X anyone can switch on a "professional category" (like "Entertainment & Recreation") with one tap, personal accounts included. PageProof used to call any account with one of these a Business. Now the category alone counts for nothing (unless it says creator, news, fan page or organisation): an X account is only Business with a gold check, a registered company, or two or more real business signs (a shop category, its own website, a business word, an email or phone). A personal account like this is now a Person and is hidden until you tick "Show personal accounts". Accounts already read are sorted again without a new read.
