title: The photo checker takes a second look, gives Google's exact matches a second chance, and learns from your answers
news: Close "Same photo" calls are now checked a second time at full size before they reach Copies found, and links Google lists as "Exact matches" are never thrown out without a full-size check. Every time you remove a Same photo or press "It is a copy", a small pair is kept in Documents\PageProof\checker-tests (this computer only) and the Checker test runs on those real pairs too. How PageProof is doing shows where the checker goes wrong by kind of site, and every Evidence report gets a second page showing the match.
timeline: Second look for close calls, second chance for exact matches, your own real checker test set, checker vs your answers by kind of site, match picture in the Evidence report
feature 6: **Second look for close calls.** A Same photo with a thin margin is checked again more slowly with both pictures at full size; if that disagrees it becomes Maybe or is thrown out ("Different on a closer look").
feature 6: **Second chance for exact matches.** A link Google lists under "Exact matches" that the fast check throws out is checked again at full size first. If it is still different it shows "Google said exact" in the thrown-out list.
feature 6: **Your own real test set.** Removing a Same photo or pressing "It is a copy" keeps a small pair (both pictures, at most 800 pixels) in Documents\PageProof\checker-tests, on this computer only. The Checker test runs these real pairs as well as the made-up ones.
feature 6: **Where the checker goes wrong.** How PageProof is doing shows the checker's answers next to yours for the last 30 days, by kind of site (news, social media, shops, stock photo sites, blogs).
feature 4: **The match in the Evidence report.** Page 2 shows your client's photo with their copy laid over it where it fits, the matching details marked and the shared area outlined, with one plain sentence: "N details line up across P% of the shared area."
