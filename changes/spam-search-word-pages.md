title: More spam pages caught
news: The Spam blogs filter now also catches made-up search pages named by words joined with + (like site.com/name+onlyfans+nude), and any copy on an ending used almost only by spam sites (.run, .fit, .forum, .makeup, .cfd, .sbs and similar). Real news articles, even about adult topics, are left alone.
