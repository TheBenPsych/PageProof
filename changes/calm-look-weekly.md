title: The calm look, step 16: Weekly summary
news: Evidence › Weekly summary has the new look and now uses the whole window. On top is a week picker: the arrows step one week back or forward, and a click on the week opens a list of the last weeks with how many copies each found. Six tiles in one row show photos searched, new copies found, marked stolen, licensed, removed and evidence reports, each with an up or down arrow against the week before (while a week is still running it says "vs all of last week"). A new chart shows the copies found in each of the last 8 weeks, stolen in red, and a click on a bar opens that week. By client is now a table you can sort by clicking a column heading; it shows the top 14 with Show all, and a click on a client opens its copies in Copies found. Under it one line names the clients that were not searched that week. On the right are Every photo searched once and the week's evidence reports, each with its case number or a yellow No case number. Save as PDF now makes the same summary on paper: the six tiles, the 8 week chart, every client (on more pages when needed), first searches and the evidence reports, with who made it and page numbers.
