news: When a copy is found, PageProof checks once whether its website still exists. If the address leads nowhere (like a site that was shut down), the copy gets a grey Site gone tag and is hidden along with the spam blogs. Show them brings them back. Copies you already have are checked a few dozen at a time while you use Copies found.
