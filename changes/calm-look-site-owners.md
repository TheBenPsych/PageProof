title: The calm look, step 9: Site owners moves under Copies found
news: Site owners now lives in the menu under Copies found and has the new look. Two tabs: Owners, and Look up a site (the old Who's behind this site page). The owner list has columns for Sites, Copies, New and Stolen, a find box, a pick of kinds (media groups, same company, linked by an ID, sub-sites, has Stolen) and a sort. The chosen owner shows four tiles you can click to see those copies, why PageProof thinks they are one owner with the proof next to each reason, their sites in a table with Look up on each, what their sites say grouped without repeats, and which clients they copied. New: Copy owner details copies all of that as plain text. Fixed: initials no longer read "G(" for names with brackets, and owners linked by an Analytics or ad ID are no longer called sub-sites.
