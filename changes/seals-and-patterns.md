title: Seals, crests and patterns no longer count as copies
news: A different photo that only shares a seal on a podium, a crest on the wall, a small corner of the picture, or a pattern like a chain-link fence or a checkered floor is now called "Different photo" with a low %, in new searches, in Copies found (unanswered copies are checked once more) and in Compare.
timeline: Seals, crests, small slices and patterns (fences, floors) no longer count as copies
feature 3: **Only a seal, a slice or a pattern.** Different photos that share only a podium seal or wall crest, only a small slice of each picture, or only a repeating pattern (fence, checkered floor, tiles) are called "Different photo" and show a low %.
